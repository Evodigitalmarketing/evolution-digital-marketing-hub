Overview

About Evolution Digital Marketing

Evolution Digital Marketing is a Winnipeg digital marketing agency founded in 2013 by Steve Erickson. We help local and regional service businesses across Canada get found on Google Search, Google Maps and the new AI-driven search tools.

Our work is built on local SEO: Google Business Profile optimization, citation building, entity optimization and location-based content that earns map-pack visibility. Alongside that we build SEO-led websites and run paid advertising, social media marketing and reputation management.

As search moves toward AI answers, we help businesses get cited and recommended in Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT and other AI assistants through answer engine optimization (AEO) and generative engine optimization (GEO).

Current count: 10 supporting branded properties plus core website and social assets.
